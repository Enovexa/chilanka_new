About Us

Celebrating Dance, Culture & Tradition

Welcome to Chilanka School of Indian Dance, a vibrant dance academy in Brisbane, Australia, dedicated to preserving and celebrating the rich heritage of Indian dance and culture. We bring together tradition, rhythm, creativity, and artistic expression in a welcoming environment where students of all ages can discover their passion for dance and develop their talents.

With five branches across Brisbane, Chilanka is passionate about nurturing aspiring dancers, building confidence, and inspiring the next generation through dedicated training and meaningful artistic experiences. Our involvement in community celebrations, Indian cultural festivals, and multicultural events provides students with valuable opportunities to perform on stage, showcase their abilities, and strengthen their connection to their cultural roots.

At Chilanka, we believe dance is more than an art form — it is a beautiful expression of identity, discipline, storytelling, and community spirit. By preserving traditional art forms while encouraging creativity and individuality, we aim to create a space where every student can grow, express themselves, and experience the joy of performing. Our mission is to keep the beauty of Indian dance traditions alive while celebrating cultural diversity and inspiring generations to come.

Our Mission & Vision

Preserving Tradition, Inspiring Generations

Our Mission

To inspire and nurture dancers of all ages through quality dance education, celebrate cultural diversity, and preserve the rich heritage of Indian dance. We strive to build confidence, encourage artistic expression, and create meaningful opportunities for students to grow through learning, cultural participation, and stage performances.

Our Vision

Behind Chilanka

Meet Your Instructor

Joby Kalabhavan teaches every class and looks after everything at Chilanka, bringing years of training, performance experience and a love for sharing Indian dance with every student.

Joby Kalabhavan

Professional Choreographer | Dance Instructor | Stage Performer

Joby Kalabhavan is an accomplished choreographer, dance instructor, and stage performer with over 30 years of excellence in dance and performing arts and an impressive international career. Having spent 15 years with the prestigious Cochin Kalabhavan, he has performed on nearly 1,000 stages across 26 countries, bringing his passion for dance to audiences worldwide. In Kerala, he has worked extensively with schools and colleges, guiding students to achieve first-place honours in state-level youth festivals and university competitions. His expertise also extends to celebrity training and music album choreography.

Now based in Brisbane, Australia, Joby continues to inspire aspiring dancers through five dance studio locations, offering professional training in Bharatanatyam, Semiclassical, Bollywood, Western, and Folk Dance, including traditional art forms such as Margamkali and Thiruvathira. Driven by a passion for preserving cultural heritage while embracing modern dance styles, his mission is to nurture talent, build confidence, and help every student discover the joy of dance.

Our Journey

What began as a small circle of dancers in Brisbane has grown into a community united by tradition, rhythm and the joy of performing.

  1. 2023

    Where it all began

    Chilanka School of Dance opens its doors in Brisbane, with a simple wish: to share classical Indian dance with a welcoming, close-knit community.

  2. Soon after

    Our first stage

    Our first annual showcase gives students the chance to perform before family and friends, turning months of practice into applause.

  3. Along the way

    Into the community

    Students take part in community cultural festivals, carrying the beauty of Indian dance to new audiences across Brisbane.

  4. Today

    A growing dance family

    With over 10,000 students trained, 1,000+ stage performances and a team of devoted instructors, our journey continues with every new dancer who joins us.
