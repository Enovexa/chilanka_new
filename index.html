Celebrate Culture, Discover Your Passion for Dance

Welcome to Chilanka School of Indian Dance, a vibrant dance academy in Brisbane, Australia, where culture, tradition, and artistic expression come together. We celebrate the beauty of Indian dance through Bharatanatyam, Semiclassical Dance, Bollywood Dance, Western Dance, and traditional folk art forms such as Margamkali and Thiruvathira. With five branches across Brisbane, we welcome children and adults of all experience levels to discover their passion for dance in a friendly and inspiring environment.

At Chilanka, dance is more than movement—it is a celebration of heritage, creativity, and self-expression. Through dedicated training, cultural programs, and exciting stage performances, we help students build confidence, develop their skills, and showcase their talents. Our mission is to preserve the richness of Indian dance traditions while inspiring the next generation of dancers to embrace their culture, express themselves, and shine on stage.
